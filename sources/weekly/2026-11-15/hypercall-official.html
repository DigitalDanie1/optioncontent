Why a trading championship

Hypercall is built for traders who want to express a view on options with real depth and clear pricing. ANT options are now live, and a competition is the best way to put them in front of the people who will use them most. Everyone starts at zero. The leaderboard shows who is trading well, and the rules reward realized profit, not noise.

How rankings work

Every eligible trader is ranked by a single score:

Score = Positive Realized PnL × Volume Multiplier

Realized profit is the foundation. Trading volume then boosts your score through three tiers:

TierQualifying volumeMultiplier
1$1,0001x
2$10,0001.5x
3$100,0002x

The multiplier applies once your qualifying volume reaches each tier. A trader who reaches $10,000 in qualifying volume scores 1.5x. A trader who reaches $100,000 scores 2x.

What counts as qualifying volume

Qualifying volume is the lower of the underlying notional or 300× the premium paid. This keeps the volume tiers tied to real trading activity.

What counts toward competition PnL

  • Realized PnL from closing fills during the competition.
  • Net settlement PnL from ANT options with expiry inside the competition window.

Unrealized gains, open-position marks, deposits and withdrawals do not count.

Eligibility

To be eligible for a prize, you must:

  • Finish with positive competition PnL.
  • Generate at least $1,000 in qualifying ANT options volume.
  • Trade during the official competition window, November 11 to December 2, 2026.

Market makers are excluded. Wash trading and any other manipulative activity are not allowed and will be disqualified. Only eligible traders can receive prizes.
